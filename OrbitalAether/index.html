Part 1: Privacy Policy for Orbital Aether

Maksym Didukh ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Orbital Aether mobile application (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Game.

1. No Data Collection and Transmission

We believe in privacy by design. Orbital Aether is a 100% offline, local arcade runner game.

  • We do not collect, store, log, or share any Personal Data (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, unique device identifiers, or location data).
  • The Game does not require Internet access and operates completely without connecting to any external network or servers.

2. Permissions & Device Features

The Game runs with zero system permissions requested. It does not access your camera, contacts, microphone, photos, storage, or geographic location. Touch inputs (screen taps) are processed strictly in real-time on your device solely to control the local gameplay mechanics.

3. No Third-Party Services, Analytics, or Advertising

To ensure a fully private experience, the Game does not integrate any third-party SDKs, libraries, or external services.

  • No Analytics: We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, or any other user-tracking behavior frameworks.
  • No Advertisements: The Game contains no ad integrations (such as Google AdMob) or advertising identifiers.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Game does not use cookies, web beacons, pixels, or any other tracking technologies to monitor your activity.

5. Children's Privacy

Because Orbital Aether does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The Game is safe for users of all ages.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
